## Request-form implications

- Which fields are required to get a usable quote? (category, area/pincode, photos, budget, urgency)
- Which fields do customers refuse or misunderstand?
- Where does the customer want to send photos — WhatsApp, web, or both?
- Does the customer expect a site visit before a quote?
- What budget language do they use: “approximately”, “maximum”, “not sure”, “tell me”?
- What makes a request feel too exposed to share?

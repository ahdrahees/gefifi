# Acquisition Outreach Scripts

> **Use with:** [acquisition-plan.md](../../plan/made-by-opencode/acquisition-plan.md)  
> **Privacy:** Never paste names, phone numbers, exact addresses, document images, or chat screenshots into the repo. Working data goes in `docs/research/private/` (gitignored) or the CRM.

---

## 1. Ground rules

- **Call first.** Do not send a cold bulk WhatsApp message. Ask permission before adding a provider to a WhatsApp follow-up list.
- **One-to-one.** No broadcasts, no groups, no forwarded lists.
- **Identify yourself.** Say your name, that you are starting a local service, and that you are not selling anything on the call.
- **Respect the answer.** “No” ends the call. Record it and move on. Call only numbers published for the business, and stop if asked.
- **Do not record calls** without the other person's consent.
- **No money in this sprint.** Do not collect a fee, deposit, or commission.
- **No Aadhaar.** Do not ask for or store Aadhaar.
- **No exact customer address.** Use area and pincode only.
- **Do not claim “verified”** before a provider has actually been verified. In this sprint, say “local providers”.
- **Log consent.** Record source, date, and what the person agreed to.

## 2. Provider call script (under 6 minutes)

> “Namaste, I’m `[name]` from Gefifi. We’re starting a service in `[area]` for `[category]` work. I’m not selling anything today.
>
> I’m looking for 10 good `[plumbers/electricians/painters]` who can respond to a customer request within 24 hours.
>
> Can I ask: how do you currently get work?”

Then:

1. “What makes a lead worth your time?”
2. “If I send you a job in `[area]` with photos and a budget, would you quote within 24 hours?”
3. “What would you need from me to trust it?”
4. “Can I send you the first one on WhatsApp?”

**Close:** “If I send a written confirmation on WhatsApp, will you reply ‘yes, I will quote within 24 hours’ so I can count you in?”

Do not pitch a subscription on the first call. Do not promise leads you do not have.

## 3. Provider WhatsApp messages

### 3.1 Permission message (after the call)

> Namaste `[name]`, this is `[founder name]` from Gefifi. We spoke about `[category]` work in `[area]`.  
> I’ll send at most one message per request, only when it matches your area and category.  
> I’ll keep your number private and won’t add you to any group.  
> Reply **STOP** if you do not want these messages.  
> Is it okay to send the first request here?

### 3.2 Written commitment request

> Thanks for the call. To count you in, please reply:  
> **“I will quote within 24 hours for `[category]` work in `[area]`.”**  
> This is not a subscription and there is no fee today. You can stop at any time.

### 3.3 First request message (only with customer consent)

> `[category]` job in `[area]`, pincode `[pincode]`.  
> Work: `[short description]`.  
> Photos: `[count]`.  
> Budget: `[range or “not sure”]`.  
> Preferred visit: `[this week / this month]`.  
> Can you quote within 24 hours? Reply **YES** to confirm or **NO** if it is not a fit.

Do not include the customer’s name, phone number, or exact address.

### 3.4 Follow-up after 48 hours

> Just checking once: did this `[category]` job in `[area]` fit? A simple **yes/no** is enough. If yes, I’ll keep sending only matching jobs.

One follow-up. Do not nag.

## 4. Customer request collection

Use a warm introduction where possible: apartment group, society committee, friend, colleague, hardware/paint shop, or the founder’s own home or office.

> Namaste, I’m `[name]` from Gefifi. We’re helping homeowners in `[area]` get 2–3 quotes from local providers for `[category]` work.  
> This is not a sales call. I’m looking for real jobs to test the service.  
> If you have something that needs doing, can I ask a few questions and collect 2–5 photos?  
> I will not share your exact address. I’ll share the job details and photos only with providers who might quote it. A provider can visit only if you agree.

A request is real only if the customer will let a provider visit or quote. “I might renovate next year” is not a request.

---

## 5. Customer interview script

> “What broke or needs doing?”  
> “How have you handled this before?”  
> “What did you pay, and how did you choose the person?”  
> “What worried you most — price, trust, timing, or quality?”  
> “Would you share photos and an approximate area to get quotes?”  
> “Would you let a provider visit before you choose?”  
> “Would knowing a provider's documents were checked change your decision?”  
> “Would you sign a simple agreement and pay milestones directly to the provider?”  
> “What would make you not use this?”

Listen for trust, not features. Write down the customer’s exact words.

---

## 6. Pricing questions

Ask after the provider has described how they get work. Do not lead with price.

- “If Gefifi brought you a job worth ₹20,000, what would a fair fee be?”
- “Would you pay ₹100–300 per qualified lead, or a percentage when the job is won?”
- “Would you pay a monthly subscription for 10 leads?”
- “Would you charge the customer more if you paid a commission?”

Record the answer, category, and provider size. The working hypothesis is free for 90 days, then a 5–8% success fee or a ₹100–300 qualified-lead fee.

---

## 7. Consent log

For every person added to a follow-up list, record in the private tracker:

- consent source (call, in-person, referral, inbound);
- consent date and time;
- what they agreed to receive (request alerts, follow-ups, product updates);
- opt-out date, if any;
- who recorded it.

Do not add anyone to a marketing broadcast without explicit opt-in. A request alert is not the same as a product newsletter.

---

## 8. Daily and weekly rhythm

**Daily**

- 5–10 provider calls.
- 2–3 customer conversations.
- Log every outcome the same day.
- Send one real request to a committed provider when a matching request exists.

**Weekly**

- Update the gate scoreboard.
- Review the top objections and buying triggers.
- Check response times from the 10 manual sends.
- Book one site visit or ride-along.
- Decide one thing to narrow or change.

**Do not start v3 code until the gate in the acquisition plan passes.**
